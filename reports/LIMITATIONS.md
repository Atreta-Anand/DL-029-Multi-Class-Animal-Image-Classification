# Limitations
- Dataset source and license are pending verification.
- No real model training or test metrics are available yet.
- SHA-256 detects exact duplicates, not perceptual duplicates.
- Stratified random split cannot guarantee source-level independence.
- Background/lighting/pose generalization needs separate genuine test images.
- Softmax scores are not calibrated correctness probabilities.
- CPU training can be slow; record actual settings and results.
